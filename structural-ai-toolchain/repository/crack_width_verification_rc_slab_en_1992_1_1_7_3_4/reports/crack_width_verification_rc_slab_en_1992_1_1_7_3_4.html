Crack Width Verification - RC Slab (EN 1992-1-1 7.3.4)

PurposeCharacteristic crack width for a reinforced concrete slab under quasi-permanent loading.

1Input Parameters

SymbolValueUnitDescription
1000mmSection width (per metre strip)
300mmSection depth
260mmEffective depth
30mmNominal concrete cover
16mmBar diameter
1005mm^2Tension reinforcement area (5 H16)
45kNmQuasi-permanent bending moment
200000MPaSteel modulus of elasticity
33000MPaConcrete secant modulus
2.9MPaEffective concrete tensile strength
0.4-Load duration factor (long term)
0.8-Bond factor (high-bond bars)
0.5-Strain distribution factor (bending)
3.4-Code coefficient
0.425-Code coefficient
0.3mmAllowable crack width (XC exposure)

2Calculation

Modular ratio E_s/E_cm
alpha_e = E_s/E_cm = 6.061
Neutral axis depth (cracked section)
x = (alpha_e*A_s/b)*(sqrt(1+2*b*d/(alpha_e*A_s))-1) = 50.52 mm
Internal lever arm
z = d-x/3 = 243.2 mm
Steel stress under quasi-permanent loads
sigma_s = M_qp*1000000/(z*A_s) = 184.1 MPa
Effective tension zone height
h_c_eff = min(2.5*(h-d),(h-x)/3,h/2) = 83.16 mm
Effective reinforcement ratio
rho_p_eff = A_s/(b*h_c_eff) = 0.01208
Maximum crack spacing
s_r_max = k_3*c_nom+k_1*k_2*k_4*phi/rho_p_eff = 327.1 mm
Mean strain difference (eps_sm - eps_cm)
eps_diff = max((sigma_s-k_t*f_ct_eff/rho_p_eff*(1+alpha_e*rho_p_eff))/E_s,0.6*sigma_s/E_s) = 0.0005524
Characteristic crack width
w_k = s_r_max*eps_diff = 0.1807 mm

3Verification

OK
