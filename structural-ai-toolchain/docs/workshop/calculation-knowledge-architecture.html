Calculation Knowledge Architecture

StatusIssued for workshop
RevisionF
Date2026-11-15

Problem

  • Thousands of calculations in Excel, Mathcad, Python and on paper are rebuilt on every project.
  • Copies drift, fixes never propagate, and nobody knows which version to trust or whether it was checked.
  • Collecting our tools has failed before: reviewing every variation, QA'ing it and learning to use it was a monstrous effort, with QA marks nobody could fully rely on.

Objective

Small, verified calculation objects that every tool, workflow and engineer can use. The design codes are written as objects first; AI then reads, converts and distils our own calculations against them, so every source becomes a benchmark and every simplification a documented, explained case. The result is a version-controlled knowledge base that maps what we know, feeds Excel, reports and AI assistants from one logic, and improves with every project that uses it. We don't build tools; we build the pieces.

General concerns

Scope

Bridges, on major projects at most; other groups join later. Within that scope aim for volume, not a small curated set. We build granular pieces, not tools.

Feasibility

Collecting tools failed because people did all the work. Now AI reads, converts and writes, and pieces exist (mcad2py, structuralcodes). The hard parts are distilling messy files and readable output.

Scalability

Effort scales worst in distilling thousands of messy files. Human review must stay light or it becomes the bottleneck. Each new model can re-check the whole base.

Adoption

The biggest risk is a system nobody uses. Logic must drop into Excel and people's own workflows, and quick work must flow back. Everyone keeps their local tools, on one shared basis.

Investment

The payoff is quality, not hours: a benchmark we QA'd ourselves for every AI tool, and independent QA even when SOFiSTiK or FAGUS design. Prove it with a minimum viable product first.

Why now

AI does the heavy lifting. With our own benchmarked pieces as their source, Copilot, RamGPT and new tools give traceable, repeatable results instead of hallucinations.

What has to be built, and how

Ingestion · 1

Retrieval and curation

Dump whole folders without sorting and let AI sort, link and deduplicate them. Don't rely on metadata.

Calculations sit in SharePoint, ProjectWise, local drives, PCs and decades of paper; the metadata is messy.

Volume may bring sources that never agree, and AI may be too weak or too costly to process it all.

NDA and permission status is unknown: scanning may open and tag material that should stay private.

Should each logic keep a record of its sources, without copying files to new servers?

  • Start with what is already accessible: SharePoint is easy with Copilot, ProjectWise is painful. high
  • Volume plus consensus instead of hand curation, with capacity and cost measured on a pilot first. low
  • Link to sources where they live (path and hash); store what is cheapest for AI to retrieve. medium
  • MVP: exact duplicates dropped; a PDF recognised as the print of a worksheet and used only as a benchmark.
HighAccess rights and NDAs, not technology, are the work.
Ingestion · 2

Conversion

Readers for every format. Every formula is re-run and must reproduce the value the file shows.

Mathcad sheets, old and Prime, are hard to read for people and AI alike.

Excel files hide hard-coded numbers, hidden sheets, Goal Seek, Solver, VBA and broken links.

Much logic survives only in PDF printouts or on handwritten pages.

A parser that keeps only the formulas loses the context, assumptions and edge cases.

  • mcad2py converts Mathcad to runnable Python with units. high
  • Excel formulas extracted as text and recreated; files that won't run are flagged, not guessed. medium
  • PDFs accepted: the recreated logic must reproduce the printed output from the printed input. medium
  • Tag everything: labels, clauses, headings and cases; every input and output becomes a test case. medium
  • MVP: Excel 1,040 of 1,040 formulas, Mathcad 84 of 84 plus 80 of 80 stored results reproduced.
LowReliability is measured file by file, not assumed.
Distillation · 3

Code seed and decomposition

Seed first: the code clauses are written as objects before any extraction; the files then act as benchmarks that expose errors and add edge cases.

Extracting logic from scratch out of hundreds of messy files is slow, token-heavy and error-prone.

Project assumptions and simplifications multiply the approaches to one problem, in several languages.

Some checks are more than formulas: interaction diagrams, multi-step procedures, 3D geometry.

The same logic gets duplicated across objects, topics and codes.

  • Code seed for EC2, EC3 and EC4: 146 objects, each starting as “not yet checked”. medium
  • AI reads the context and matches sources despite their simplifications; each simplification becomes a note juniors see. low
  • Objects call objects, so a tool is a larger object built from smaller ones. low
  • Objects as small as possible, taxonomy from the clause structure; a later code links to an existing object instead of copying it. high
HighNeeds engineering understanding; the seed makes it tractable.
Distillation · 4

Consolidation against the code

Each source is compared with the seeded object, not with the other sources: as code, simplified (with why and with what effect), different, or project-specific.

AI may not tell inputs, outputs and assumptions apart across sources.

Agreement needs a tolerance, and codes, editions and national annexes differ.

Experts may never settle on one approach, and most topics have one source only.

  • Compare against the code object, so sources never have to agree with each other. low
  • 0 % for closed-form checks, every difference explained; about 1 % for numerical ones. Each code generation, national annex and guideline is a branch. medium
  • Large differences are investigated first: an input read from another calculation, per-metre against full width, a unit, one different value. What stays unexplained becomes a question for an engineer. medium
  • An expert group decides, keeping alternatives; single-source topics are published as “seen once”. low

Human review. An engineer answers the questions the distillation cannot, and settles real disagreements. Each answer becomes design background of the object.

HighA new method; the code seed removes most of the guesswork.
Knowledge base · 5

Object generation

One specification per object: formula, clause, units, conditions and design background. Python, Excel, Mathcad and report equations are generated from it, never written by hand.

There are no shared coding conventions; everyone codes their own way.

Formal QA marks need a dedicated checker and budget, and still miss errors; no team exists to build benchmarks.

  • Open-source libraries such as structuralcodes as independent references. high
  • Real project use is the benchmark; every value a source shows is re-run, and every fix is pushed back. medium
  • A version fingerprint in every file; one command tells whether a file still carries the current logic.

Human review. Sign-off confirms an object is sound; whoever uses it stays responsible.

LowGeneration is mechanical; trust comes from the benchmarks.
Knowledge base · 6

Repository and search

A versioned knowledge base whose objects drop into people's own Excel sheets and workflows, with quick Excel work flowing back.

Most people want to use the logic, not Python, and web apps are stiff.

An adapted copy fed back looks “wrong”, and non-coders can't extend a module.

AI assistants produce calculations that can't be traced and may hallucinate.

Search may return the wrong object, and no one owns a topic.

  • Two-way bridge: Excel calculators out (named ranges or cell references), any workbook back in and classified. low
  • Project variants stored as branches, not corrections; AI turns an Excel extension into an object. low
  • The repository as the source for Copilot and RamGPT: tool definitions with every step and clause. medium
  • The engineer always QAs; anyone can flag, gatekeepers keep it healthy, AI proposes fixes. medium
MediumHosting is standard; adoption is the risk.
Use and maintenance · 7

Self-documenting calculations

A report template set up once and run over any number of cases: the governing case written out, a summary table, charts and remarks. PDF with bookmarks, or Word with native equations.

Code with ifs and loops doesn't read like a hand calculation; an earlier LaTeX wrapper on SymPy was inflexible.

Reports must meet the company standard: equation look, sketches, inline clauses, colour-coded utilisation, QA block.

Lessons, warnings and competing interpretations stay in individual heads.

  • Equations printed from the formula tree, not from the code: MathML, Word equations, LaTeX. medium
  • Templates filled in an Excel form or in the web page, never in code: a locked look, chosen content. low
  • Design guides generated from the objects' background: scope, pitfalls, where interpretations differ. medium
HighThe company standard and sketches are the open work.
Use and maintenance · 8

Maintenance

Health checks, flags and fixes. The AI writes and repairs; gatekeepers approve; everything is versioned.

No one owns, hosts or funds the system or its gatekeepers.

Someone flags an error and needs the fix now; waiting for an approver blocks them.

Fixes and code revisions change objects already used on projects.

Mistakes already in the knowledge base go unnoticed; people may not bother to contribute.

  • Hosting and sizing to the tech department; automate as much as possible. low
  • The fix is deployed as an unapproved copy until the person responsible reviews it. medium
  • Users of a version are notified of updates; old and new codes sit side by side. medium
  • Health checks re-run over the whole base with each more capable model. high
  • Finished calculations go into a folder the AI absorbs and checks. medium

Human review. Gatekeepers approve corrections and new components; a short meeting settles disagreements.

MediumOwnership and funding more than technology.

Worked example: the MVP on three real files

One Mathcad worksheet, its PDF print and one Excel workbook went through the whole chain in about five seconds.

    Ingestion

    Every formula re-runs to the value in its file; the PDF is recognised as a print of the worksheet.

  2. 2
    Code seed

    146 objects from EC2, EC3 and EC4, with the full expressions: f_cd = α_cc·f_ck/γ_C, not the f_ck/γ_C the sheets write.

  3. 3
    Against the code

    All 86 source formulas for V_Rd,c are simplifications: the lower bound V_Rd,c,min is left out (it governs in 22 of 80 cases, on the safe side) and k₁σ_cp is dropped where σ_cp ≠ 0 (8 cases, unsafe). γ_C = 1.3775 is used 72 times.

  4. 4
    Understanding

    A 540× difference was traced to one spreadsheet column holding two calculations; the lookup was fixed rather than tolerated. Per-metre working became context on the object: “usually calculated per metre width for slabs”.

  5. 5
    Objects

    31 checked (the code's own expression, confirmed by an independent library or by source values) and 115 not yet checked, each with design background and sources.

    Use

    Web calculator, Excel calculator, Mathcad worksheet and report templates (PDF with bookmarks, Word); one command confirms each file carries the current logic.

  7. 7
    Maintenance

    The wider seed immediately flagged f_ywd,ef = 250 + 0.2·d in the workbook against 250 + 0.25·d in the code. Every health check re-runs the 672 stored benchmarks.

Pressing questions, with a first answer

Logic we don't own

Can we take design logic from other firms' reports, or from codes we don't hold a licence for?

Formulas and methods are not protected as such; the text of standards and reports is. Store the logic in our own words with clause references, never copied text, and keep client reports under their NDA: logic in, project data out, a link to the file.

Metadata

What metadata is realistic or necessary to harvest?

Only what is cheap and reliable: file hash, a link to where it lives, format, date, project number from the path, code and edition referenced; per value its label, unit, clause, heading and location. Author and QA status are rarely reliable.

Specification format

What format is best for the specifications and the code blocks?

A plain-text specification per object (YAML now, JSON or a database later) with formulas in a small arithmetic language; code, Excel, Mathcad and equations are generated from it. Git gives history and review.

Reports and hosting

How hard is report formatting, with which tools, hosted where?

One template, filled in an Excel form or the web page; output as PDF with bookmarks, or Word with native equations for editing. Knowledge base in Git (Azure DevOps or GitHub), calculator on SharePoint or Azure Static Web Apps, checks in CI.

People

How many humans, and where?

A part-time lead and one or two developers; one gatekeeper per code part (five to eight engineers, a few hours a month); IT for hosting. Everyone else uses, flags and answers questions.

Distilled files

Do we keep the files we distilled, or throw them away?

Keep a manifest (hash, link, date, what was extracted) plus the extracted statements and benchmark numbers, which are small. Not the files: they stay where they live, and the hash proves what was used.

Sharing

How do we share it with everyone easily?

The calculator is one HTML file: a Teams tab or SharePoint page. Excel calculators and reports travel as files; a Python package on an internal feed; tool definitions for Copilot.

Access and curation

Curate by hand, open ProjectWise and SharePoint safely, or just dump logic in a folder? Access and QA are a nightmare.

Don't curate by hand. A drop folder for finished calculations plus read-only access to selected SharePoint sites; ProjectWise exports per project when needed. QA by arithmetic, through benchmarks, not by reading files.

Parser reliability

How reliable are the parsers, and do they need improving?

Measured per file: every formula is re-run against the value the file shows. Weak spots are VBA, Goal Seek, lookups, iteration and scans; they are flagged, not guessed, and improved as volume shows what matters.

Sampling

How much sampling is needed? Millions of variations per file? How is it tracked?

Not millions: 200 random points per object against an independent library, plus every value the sources show. Next step: sample both sides of every min, max and condition on purpose. Counts are stored per object and re-run by each health check.

Who updates

Who updates the blocks: a responsible bottleneck, or AI keeping everything healthy?

The AI writes and fixes; every change is a proposal with its evidence (benchmarks re-run, differences explained); a topic gatekeeper approves. The consensus and confidence rules are public, like an AI-written encyclopedia with visible rules and history.

Software-agnostic

What do software-agnostic specifications buy us, and do they lose information?

The specification outlives the software: if Python is replaced, the generators change, not the knowledge. It holds everything the MVP needs; iteration, tables and plots need extensions to the format.
